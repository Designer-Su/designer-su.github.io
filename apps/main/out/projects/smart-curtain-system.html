Product Design

Smart Curtain System

A smart curtain system that lets workers adjust privacy, comfort, and focus through physical space control and a mobile app.

Year
2024
Duration
4 Months
Role
Product Designer (UX/UI, Research, Concept, Prototype)

Problem

Open offices and hybrid workplaces often give users little control over privacy, noise, light, and temperature. This makes focus and comfort harder to maintain.

Solution

  • Spatial Control: A modular curtain system that divides or connects work zones.
  • Environmental Control: Light, temperature, and acoustic settings that support focus and comfort.
  • App Control: A mobile interface for adjusting the workspace in real time.

Impact

  • Improved Perceived Privacy: Helped users create clearer personal boundaries in open and shared work environments.
  • Increased Workspace Adaptability: Enabled spaces to shift between focus, collaboration, and comfort modes through modular physical control.
  • Integrated Physical + Digital UX: Connected material, movement, and app-based environmental control into one coherent workspace experience.
